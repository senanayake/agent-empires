# AE-011 formative readback protocol

Status: prepared before participant collection; no participants supplied or recruited.
Question, authority and threshold: [AE-011](../../../docs/mvp-campaign.md#ae-011--test-whether-a-causal-trace-explains-the-first-story).
This protocol tests explanation, not enjoyment, calibration or architecture.

## Materials and reproducibility

Use CPython 3.12.13 and the locked checkout environment. Generate once:

```bash
python3 experiments/trace_report.py --packet-dir research/experiments/AE-011/packet
python3 experiments/trace_report.py --verify-against research/experiments/AE-011/packet
```

The source is AE-010's reviewed `causal-traces.json`. Case A is its predeclared
`boundary/harmful_human`; B is `boundary/harmless_unsupported`. Both select the
same new-key retry plan without authority/evidence support; A later observes one
duplicate and B none. They are deliberately chosen boundary examples, not a
random cohort, representative workload or proof that the choice always harms.
No world parameters, histories or participant answers are regenerated by this tool.
The separate `packet/audit/manifest.json` binds the source SHA-256 and case mapping.

Hand readers ONLY `packet/reader/`. Keep this protocol, scoring rubric, source
case names and `packet/audit/` private until answers are frozen. The delivery-desk
projection shows declared goals/work, required/selected/revised choices with their
recorded provenance, gap categories, pinned delivery versions, assurance statuses
and delayed `OutcomeObserved` records. All knowledge holdings/propositions,
grants, raw attempts, incidents, world outcomes, command/entity metadata, freeform
rationale, missing-information references and unrecognized event types are excluded.
Even an accessible holding of another actor is not desk access. No new actor
snapshot or general permission system is claimed. Missing parents are marked
unavailable without inventing an edge across them. Typed audit edges remain
separate: association is not a damage cause. Observation counts establish a
recorded consequence, not universal incorrectness or retrospective justification.

Before-feedback view ends at shipping tick 1; after-feedback view at tick 8. The physical
outcome/incident at tick 3 is private until its observation arrives at tick 8.
All six decisions lack support in these fixtures: do not pretend only idempotency
is unsupported. The full Sarah-replacement/knowledge-loss story is absent from
these histories; do not invent it. That limits this exercise to the implemented
inference -> delivery -> delayed-observation chain.

## Collection

Recruit exactly three distinct actual readers unfamiliar with the project/thesis
and prior traces. Ask prior familiarity and obtain permission for anonymized
verbatim records. Decliners are not participants. Assign distinct anonymized IDs
only after actual participation; record screening and consent without names or
contact details. Do not use coding agents, actor policies or worker opinions as
participants. Do not send invitations from this worker; operator recruitment is
external.

Give the neutral instructions in `packet/reader/instructions.md`. Present A-before,
record its first answer; then A-after, record all answers verbatim. Freeze primary
answers before B-before/B-after and the comparison. Do not mention the thesis,
unsupported decisions, the desired intervention or correct answers. Do not coach,
fill silence or explain jargon until primary answers are frozen. Record any
clarification verbatim and mark prompted responses; they cannot satisfy the primary
criterion. Record timing descriptively, without a speed acceptance threshold.

Copy `response-template.md` separately for each actual session. Preserve confusion,
dissent, wrong predictions and skipped questions verbatim. Mark quotations versus
facilitator observations explicitly. Attach material hashes and order to each
record. Keep the blank template out of participant evidence. No responses directory
or participant IDs exist at preparation time.

## Scoring, falsifiers and disposition

An actual named reviewer reads raw responses against both player and audit views.
For each of the three readers, primary success requires BOTH, unprompted in A:

1. Identify the new-operation-key/idempotency choice as lacking recorded authority
   or independent evidence, with a concrete record reference. Naming green checks
   or the duplicate alone is insufficient. Accept ordinary language; exact jargon
   is unnecessary. Record recognition of other unsupported choices separately.
2. Propose a plausible intervention connected to that gap or consequence: seek
   interface/rationale clarification and independently test ambiguous retry behavior,
   or investigate/use a stable operation key and validate it before shipping.
   Equivalent concrete alternatives count. Replacing an actor solely because of
   kind, adding more green checks against the same interpretation, or promising
   no future harm without validation does not count.

Record criteria separately, including failures, ambiguity, dissent and reviewer
rationale. B is a contrast probe: can readers separate absent support from observed
harm? Do not use B or coached corrections to rescue a failed primary score. No
conclusion is drawn before all three actual unfamiliar readers respond. At least
two of three must meet both primary criteria; this is an invented formative design
threshold (MA-16), not statistical evidence. If fewer than two qualify, preserve
negative findings, identify explanation/model failures, and propose a bounded
trace/model revision and fresh readback before C4/dependent work. Do not tune the
rubric or discard a completed negative session. Missing actual records means defer,
not failure of comprehension and not pass. A pass requires genuine response
artifacts, participant IDs, substantive review and current bound hashes.
