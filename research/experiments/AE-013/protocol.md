# AE-013 bounded learning protocol

Before implementation: exercise an omitted-retry defect, independent accessible
requirement reports, and an authorized blanket decline policy in an authored
soft-decline world. Keep goal, workload and old deployment versions fixed.
Use four observed operations as the minimum sample and an absolute abandonment
rate margin of 0.2 above the goal target before challenging a policy. These are
invented MA-09 finite-rule parameters, not a significance test. Check sample
bounds 0/1/3/4, one noisy abandonment, copied/shared roots, contradictions,
stale or inaccessible reports, wrong subjects, stale commands and delayed old
feedback. Never consume private payment outcomes as observed evidence.

Falsifiers: copied verification permits interpretation learning; weak/shared
reports gain support by repetition; delayed evidence rewrites deployed versions;
authorization implies fitness; a replacement policy is called validated before
new observed exposure. Preserve any red outputs and negative findings.

Trace all three loops with event IDs, changed object references, retained prior
history and actual conformance/fitness results. Replay the trace author in
temporary storage for byte comparison; do not overwrite reviewed evidence.
No participant, empirical-calibration or general statistical claim is tested.
