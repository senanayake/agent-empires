## Context and uncertainty

What concrete problem needs a decision? Which constraints come from the product
brief, which are hypotheses, and what evidence is missing?

## Alternatives and criteria

List the feasible alternatives, including a simpler or deferred option. Compare
them on the criteria relevant to this decision.

## Decision and consequences

State the selected scope, trade-offs, dependency direction and limits. Do not
represent a proposed choice as implemented or empirically proven.

## Evidence and validation

Link the exact experiment, test, review or user constraint. Record negative
evidence and distinguish machine checks from human/product acceptance.

## Revisit trigger

Name an observable failure, new requirement or campaign activity that would
justify revisiting the decision.
